Fabric-to-Fit · body capture test

Get measured with your phone

  1. Lean the phone against a wall on the floor. The app checks the angle.
  2. Step back about 2 metres and follow the voice until you are in view.
  3. Turn once, slowly, with your arms held out. It takes about 20 seconds.
cm ft in
Without shoes. Every other measurement is scaled from this, so measure it if you can.

Nothing is uploaded. Pictures stay on this phone until you save the capture file and choose who to send it to.

Step 1 of 4 · what to wear

Wear something that follows your body

The camera measures the outline it sees. Loose cloth adds centimetres that are not you.

✓ Good

  • Leggings or cycling shorts
  • Fitted T-shirt or vest
  • Underwear or swimwear

✕ Not for this

  • Wrapper, kaftan, agbada, boubou
  • Jeans, skirts, loose trousers
  • Jackets and hoodies
  • ✓Shoes off, long hair tied up, pockets empty.
  • ✓A plain wall behind you and light on your front help. Avoid standing in front of a bright window.
  • ✓About 2.5 metres of clear floor in front of the wall the phone leans on.
Step 2 of 4 · sound and camera

Turn your volume up

You will be 2 metres from the phone, so the app talks you through it. Play the test and raise the volume until it is clear from across the room.

Next, the phone asks to use the camera and motion sensor.

The camera sees you; the motion sensor checks how the phone is leaning. Both are used only on this phone.
